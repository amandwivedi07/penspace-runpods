A single workout rarely changes how someone looks. Reading 10 pages does not make a person knowledgeable. Saving a modest amount of money once does not create wealth. Because the immediate results of small actions are usually disappointing, people often underestimate them. They expect meaningful change to feel meaningful while it is happening. James Clear argues that habits work differently. Their power comes from accumulation. A tiny improvement repeated consistently can eventually produce an outcome far larger than the original action suggests.
